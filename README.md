# The Forgotten World

A working title for a small, atmospheric isometric pixel-art exploration and puzzle game.

## Status
An end-to-end playable journey prototype is in progress. The four areas and cliffhanger are an initial implementation pass; the full 2–3 hour content and art polish remain.

## Vision
A peaceful, mysterious, occasionally melancholic journey through an original fictional medieval fantasy world. A silent protagonist explores abandoned places, solves environmental puzzles, and uncovers traces of the world's past. Beth, a playful glowing spirit orb, is their companion.

## Current baseline
- Single-player narrative exploration and puzzle adventure; no combat.
- Isometric pixel art; full-game target of about 2–3 hours.
- One continuous journey, with four distinct areas as a planning estimate, a clear main route, short optional branches, and limited return paths.
- Observation-led puzzles with fair clues and free experimentation.
- Beth offers three hint levels. Repeated hint use leaves in-world traces and may affect Beth, later puzzles, optional discoveries, and the ending.
- The opening prototype currently spans the threshold, first ruins, and a quiet orchard, with an early mechanism puzzle in each.
- The route continues through a reflective archive to a roofless observatory, where the old inscription changes and ends on a cliffhanger.
- Soft procedural ambience and chimes support the first playable pass; production audio is still open.
- An inscription found earlier starts as gibberish and gradually animates into a hidden message near the ending. It is the first clear sign of the protagonist's connection to the world's past; its meaning is open.
- First playable slice: a small version of the crossing and Beth's discovery, with one puzzle. Placeholder writing and art are acceptable.

## Planned technology
Godot 4.7.2 stable, GDScript, Windows PC first, keyboard and controller, Git for local version history. Figma and Canva are optional planning/promotion tools. The settings screen includes ambient audio, reduced motion, larger text, and camera snap options.
