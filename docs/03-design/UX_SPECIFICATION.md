# UX Specification

## Principles

Invite curiosity, let environments guide the player without a permanent marker, make interactions clear without clutter, allow safe puzzle experimentation, and keep hints optional and in-world.

## Current behavior

- Direct eight-direction input is mapped to the isometric ground plane.
- A nearby marker and a short prompt describe available actions.
- Examine, take, activate, and item use are separate actions.
- Puzzle failures are gentle and do not consume items.
- The journal records clue IDs; Beth offers three hint levels.
- Escape opens pause. The player can resume or save and return to title.
- Camera follows, turns by quarter turns, zooms a little, and can snap rather than ease.
- Reduced motion changes Beth's bob and camera easing.

## Remaining UX work

A runtime review is still needed for controller focus, text clipping, prompt position, first-time onboarding, long-route pacing, and item discovery. Camera framing/occlusion and a fuller journal presentation are unfinished.
