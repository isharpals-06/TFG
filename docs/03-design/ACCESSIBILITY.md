# Accessibility

## Implemented in the prototype

- Keyboard and controller defaults for movement and core actions.
- Remapping for movement, run, examine, take, activate, keepsakes, journal, hints, pause, camera turns, zoom, and camera snap.
- Larger-text toggle applied to menu, journal, hint, inventory, pause, and ending text and buttons; long menu buttons widen to fit.
- Reduced-motion option that removes the traveler step cycle and Beth's bobbing/reaction motion, makes area transitions and interface panels appear without slides/fades, and speeds up camera easing. The ending inscription resolves immediately while keeping its full reading pause.
- Camera snap option.
- Context prompts, short text feedback, and puzzle symbols/shapes so essential cues are not audio-only.
- No combat, timers, or resource cost for incorrect puzzle attempts.

## Still required

- Review all menus and the full route with keyboard and controller.
- Review every remappable action and the matching on-screen prompt after a custom binding is chosen.
- Check text at every supported display scale and prevent clipping with larger text.
- Keep low-vision puzzle clues legible and distinct without color alone.
- Consider a separate UI-scale choice and check controller focus order.
- Review reduced motion across every transition/effect, including the instant inscription reveal.
- Confirm that audio is never the only route or puzzle cue.

No accessibility review has been completed in a runtime playthrough. Do not describe the game as fully accessible until this work is done.
