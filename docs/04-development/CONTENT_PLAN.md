# Content and pacing plan

## Target

Design for a first playthrough of about 2 hours 15 minutes on the main route, with optional areas adding about 25–35 minutes. These are future pacing targets, not measured results. The existing route has not had a complete playthrough and remains shorter than the 2–3 hour goal.

| Chapter | Main spaces | First-play target | Content needed before the target is credible |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1. The buried crossing | Crossing, ruins | 15 min | Let players learn movement, observe the inscription, wake Beth, and solve the first stone sequence at an unhurried pace. Add a few discoverable environmental details without adding tutorial popups. |
| 2. Water returns | Orchard, waterworks | 20 min | Build a connected investigation from dry channels to the wheel room. Give each room multiple useful observations and make the restored flow visibly change both spaces. |
| 3. A square without voices | Empty quarter, reading room | 20 min | Deepen the four-house restoration, optional study room, well return, and the village-gathering memory thread. Keep its records about the people who lived here. |
| 4. The record beneath the water | Archive, map chamber | 25 min | Expand the lens route and map sequence with room to inspect, revisit, and compare records. Keep the main route clear while rewarding the mirror shard and route ledger. |
| 5. What the reflections kept | Gallery, Bell Court | 20 min | Pace the reflected-stone puzzle and bell sequence as separate discoveries. Make the empty procession and keeper's records carry the valley's departure story. |
| 6. The last high path | Highland refuge, High Steps | 20 min | Make the refuge a real rest point with several authored discoveries. Expand the vane puzzle and hint-dependent map variant with fair, visible clues. |
| 7. Under an open sky | Observatory, inscription | 15 min | Give the chart and astrolabe optional depth, let the player approach the earlier inscription again, then end on its subtle animated message alone. |

The main chapter targets total approximately 135 minutes. Optional paths should add 25–35 minutes for players who explore them. Avoid adding empty walking distance or repeated puzzle patterns just to increase time.

## Current content status

- Twelve main spaces, nine main puzzle mechanisms, and seven optional puzzles are implemented in the local source. The High Steps also has an optional ridge-fork cairn and a short record of the trail custom.
- The village well's saved bowl → lamp → bell sequence connects three records from separate locations and reveals ordinary valley history.
- The highland refuge adds a saveable rest area and three records between Bell Court and High Steps.
- Optional records, wrong-input recovery, local saves, three hint levels, reduced motion, and a Windows export exist.
- No complete runtime playthrough or duration measurement has been performed. The 2–3 hour target remains open.

## Completion approach

1. Add authored room interactions and connective story beats where they develop an existing chapter.
2. Add a new room only when it contributes a distinct location, puzzle idea, or story beat.
3. Give every required puzzle an inspectable clue, a safe wrong-input response, visible progress, and three proportional hint levels.
4. Keep optional history independent from the traveler's identity. The observatory inscription remains the only clear sign of that connection in this release.
5. Estimate chapter time during design, then leave the target unverified until a complete playthrough is requested and performed.
