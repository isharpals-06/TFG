# User Stories

## Exploration

- As a player, I want to explore a calm isometric world at my own pace.
- As a player, I want environmental cues to encourage discovery without constant markers.
- As a player, I want optional branches to reward curiosity without blocking the main route.
- As a player, I want to revisit nearby details where the route allows.

## Puzzles and objects

- As a player, I want an interaction prompt near usable objects.
- As a player, I want to examine, take, activate, and use objects intentionally.
- As a player, I want item notes and a selection page so I can remember what I found.
- As a player, I want to exchange a non-story keepsake when my inventory is full.
- As a player, I want to experiment without losing resources on wrong attempts.
- As a player, I want puzzle progress to persist and solving to visibly change the world.

## Beth and journal

- As a player, I want Beth to react to places, objects, and hints.
- As a player, I want three hint levels so I can choose how much help I receive.
- As a player, I want repeated hint use to leave a meaningful in-world trace.
- As a player, I want clues to enter a journal without the game connecting them for me.

## Comfort and continuity

- As a player, I want to pause, save, and return to the title safely.
- As a player, I want keyboard/controller options, readable text, reduced motion, and sound settings.
- As a player, I want Continue to restore my journey and preferences.
