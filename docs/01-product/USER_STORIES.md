# User Stories

## Exploration
- As a player, I want to explore a calm isometric world at my own pace.
- As a player, I want environmental cues to lead toward discoveries without constant markers.
- As a player, I want optional branches to reward curiosity without blocking the main route.
- As a player, I want a few return paths to revisit missed details.

## Puzzles and objects
- As a player, I want a clear interaction icon for usable objects.
- As a player, I want to examine, pick up, and activate objects intentionally.
- As a player, I want to experiment without losing resources on wrong attempts.
- As a player, I want puzzle progress to persist when I leave and return.
- As a player, I want puzzle solutions to change the environment, open routes, or reveal history.

## Beth and journal
- As a player, I want Beth to react to places and objects.
- As a player, I want three optional hint levels so I can choose how much help I need.
- As a player, I want hint use to feel part of the fiction through Beth and the world.
- As a player, I want clues added automatically to an animated journal without answers being given away.
