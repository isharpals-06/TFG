# Scope

## In scope
Single-player exploration and environmental puzzles; isometric pixel art; silent protagonist with a player choice of identity/presentation; Beth; original fictional world; small inventory; animated clue journal; three-level hint system; 2–3 hour eventual target; Windows PC first; keyboard and controller.

## First playable slice
A small opening and ruins area with movement, camera, interaction, one observation-led puzzle, a visible restoration or route change, Beth, one journal entry, and a hint-consequence prototype. Temporary art and writing are allowed.

## Not in the first slice
Full four-area journey, final lore, production art/audio, publishing pipeline, monetization, online services, analytics, or mod support.

## Explicitly not planned
Combat, multiplayer, accounts, and AI/ML features.

## Scope rule
New features should strengthen calm exploration, puzzle clarity, atmosphere, or narrative. Defer features that do not serve those goals.
