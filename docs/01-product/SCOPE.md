# Scope

## Release intent

Create a complete beginning, middle, and cliffhanger ending for a calm isometric puzzle adventure, aiming for approximately 2–3 hours on a first playthrough. Keep the world small and memorable, with fair environmental puzzles, Beth, optional discoveries, and a clear main route.

## Current prototype

The current source routes through the crossing, first ruins, orchard, archive, and observatory. It includes an early rune sequence, orchard water restoration, archive lens alignment, optional keepsakes, journal clues, Beth's hints, local saves, and the inscription cliffhanger. This is a short implementation pass; the target duration and full puzzle/content scope remain outstanding.

## In scope for the full game

- Single-player exploration; no combat or time pressure.
- Original fictional medieval setting and silent protagonist with a small appearance choice.
- Isometric pixel art, original or properly licensed audio, and Windows PC distribution.
- Beth as the curious spirit companion and optional three-level hint guide.
- Item discovery, inspection, selection, compatible use, and inventory swapping.
- A clue journal that archives observations without connecting them for the player.
- Locally saved story, puzzle, journal, inventory, setting, and hint state.
- Keyboard/controller support, readable text, reduced motion, and non-audio cues.
- A subtle inscription reveal at the cliffhanger; no further explanation of the protagonist's past in this release.

## Not planned

Combat, multiplayer, accounts, online services, analytics, monetization, mod support, or generated story/gameplay AI.

## Scope rule

Add only features that strengthen calm exploration, puzzle clarity, atmosphere, or story. Keep optional content non-blocking. Do not publish until content, assets, rights, accessibility, and Windows packaging have been reviewed.
