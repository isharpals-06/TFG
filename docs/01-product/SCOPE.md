# Scope

## Release intent

Create a complete beginning, middle, and cliffhanger ending for a calm isometric puzzle adventure, aiming for approximately 2–3 hours on a first playthrough. Keep the world small and memorable, with fair environmental puzzles, Beth, optional discoveries, and a clear main route.

## Current prototype

The current source routes through twelve spaces: the crossing, first ruins, orchard, old waterworks, empty quarter, archive, map chamber, reflection gallery, bell garden, highland refuge, highland steps, and observatory, with optional lower-cistern, restored-garden, reed-causeway, and west-house reading-room branches. The lower cistern opens after the waterworks sequence and adds a saved four-turn valve pattern and water-sharing ledger without blocking the village gate. The map chamber adds a saved spring → bridge → village sequence and a ledger about communal flood-season work between the Archive and reflection gallery. The waterworks adds a four-mark wheel sequence between orchard and village; the causeway rejoins through it. Restoring the village household puzzle opens the reading room's saved three-marker puzzle and two records of ordinary village life. Solving the Archive also opens a return stair to the village well and its starless reflection clue. Three history records found across the waterworks, Archive, and bell garden unlock a saved bowl → lamp → bell sequence at the village well, which reveals the valley's last gathering. Using the mirror shard on the archive mural reveals a second, optional lens solution and hidden keeper's note after the main stair opens. In the observatory, an optional chart-guided astrolabe puzzle reveals an independent weather ledger without blocking the inscription. The game includes nine required puzzle mechanisms, seven optional puzzles, keepsakes, journal clues, Beth's hints, local saves, original generated location backgrounds, and the inscription cliffhanger. This remains an expanded prototype; the 2–3 hour target and full content scope remain outstanding.

## In scope for the full game

The optional village-well gathering puzzle becomes available after finding three ordinary-history records in separate areas. Its saved bowl → lamp → bell sequence rewards a final valley-history entry and does not affect the main route. The current prototype therefore contains seven optional puzzles; this addition does not close the 2–3 hour content gap.

- Single-player exploration; no combat or time pressure.
- Original fictional medieval setting and silent protagonist with a small appearance choice.
- Isometric pixel art, original or properly licensed audio, and Windows PC distribution.
- Beth as the curious spirit companion and optional three-level hint guide.
- Item discovery, inspection, selection, compatible use, and inventory swapping.
- A clue journal that archives observations without connecting them for the player.
- Locally saved story, puzzle, journal, inventory, setting, and hint state.
- Keyboard/controller support, readable text, reduced motion, and non-audio cues.
- A subtle inscription reveal at the cliffhanger; no further explanation of the protagonist's past in this release.

## Not planned

Combat, multiplayer, accounts, online services, analytics, monetization, mod support, or generated story/gameplay AI.

## Scope rule

Add only features that strengthen calm exploration, puzzle clarity, atmosphere, or story. Keep optional content non-blocking. Do not publish until content, assets, rights, accessibility, and Windows packaging have been reviewed.
