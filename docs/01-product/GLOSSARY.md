# Glossary
- **Area:** Distinct exploration space within the continuous journey.
- **Beth:** Small glowing spirit companion; expressive, curious, affectionate.
- **Clue journal:** Automatic animated archive; does not solve or link clues.
- **Environmental puzzle:** Puzzle solved through observation, objects, patterns, or mechanisms.
- **Hint consequence:** In-world reaction escalating with hint use and potentially affecting later content.
- **Main route:** Intended forward path through the complete story.
- **Optional branch:** Short, non-required path for extra discovery or reward.
- **Vertical slice:** Small polished sample demonstrating the intended experience.
