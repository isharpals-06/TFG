# Glossary

- **Area:** A distinct exploration space within the continuous journey.
- **Beth:** Small glowing spirit companion; expressive, curious, affectionate.
- **Clue journal:** Automatic archive of observations; it does not solve or connect clues.
- **Environmental puzzle:** A puzzle solved through observation, objects, patterns, or mechanisms.
- **Hint echo:** In-world trace that rises with Beth's hints, recedes locally after a puzzle, and persists in a small form across the journey.
- **Keepsake:** A carried object found during exploration. The medallion is a protected story item.
- **Main route:** Intended forward path through the complete story.
- **Optional discovery:** Non-blocking item, clue, memory, or restoration for a curious player.
- **Vertical slice:** A small polished sample demonstrating the intended experience.
